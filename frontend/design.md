# SkillNet Design System

## Direction
Clean, trust-first architectural SaaS language built on a unified light surface system, crisp geometric typography, and editorial photography rooted in peer-to-peer knowledge exchange.

## Color Palette
- **Primary Navy (`--color-primary`):** `#0A1F3C` — Primary headings, primary CTA buttons, focused anchor surfaces
- **Primary Hover (`--color-primary-hover`):** `#122D54` — Hover states on dark navy interactive surfaces
- **Brand Accent Emerald (`--color-accent-strong` / `--color-accent`):** `#047857` / `#059669` — High-contrast verified indicators, active states, offering badges
- **Accent Soft Surface (`--color-accent-soft`):** `#ECFDF5` — Soft highlight tiles and badges paired with `#A7F3D0` hairlines
- **Canvas (`--color-bg-light`):** `#F7F9FC` — Primary page background across marketing and workspace views
- **Tonal Band (`--color-bg-subtle`):** `#EEF2F7` — Section separation bands and recessed containers
- **Card Surface (`--color-bg-white`):** `#FFFFFF` — Elevated cards, panels, inputs
- **Ink Primary / Secondary / Muted:** `#0F172A` / `#475569` / `#64748B`
- **Hairline Border:** `#E2E8F0`

## Typography
- **Display (`--font-display`):** Plus Jakarta Sans (`500`–`800`), tight tracking (`-0.025em` to `-0.035em`)
- **Body (`--font-sans`):** DM Sans (`400`–`700`), comfortable `1.6` line-height
- **Technical / Ledger Metadata (`--font-mono`):** JetBrains Mono (`400`–`700`)

## Shape Scale
- **Cards & Containers:** `16px` (`--radius-lg`)
- **Buttons, Inputs & Rows:** `10px` (`--radius-md`)
- **Pills & Tags:** `9999px` (`--radius-full`)
